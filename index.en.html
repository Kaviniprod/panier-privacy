Keep track of what you pay

What your groceries really cost.

Panier reads your till receipts and builds each product's price history, shop by shop. You see where to buy, and what changed.

Receipts are read on your phone.

From receipt to history

  1. Photograph the receipt

    Panier reads the lines on your phone: product, quantity and price.

  2. Fix what needs fixing

    The printed total is the check. If the lines do not add up to it, Panier shows which ones to look at, and remembers your corrections.

  3. Compare

    Each purchase joins the product's history, compared by price per kilo or per litre.

Your receipts stay with you

  • On your phoneReceipts, photos and prices never leave the phone: neither we nor anyone else receives them.
  • A Google accountIt is required to open Panier, which checks it at every launch: the app does not open without a connection.
  • Very little leavesTo recognise a shop or a product, Panier may send the receipt's SIRET number to the public business registry, or a product's barcode to Open Food Facts. Never a photo, a date or your location.
