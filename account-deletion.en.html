Delete your Panier account

This page is a translation of the French text, Supprimer votre compte Panier; if the two differ, the French text prevails.

Delete now, from this page

Sign in with the Google account you use for Panier: you will see its address, then confirm. The account is deleted at once, with no reply to wait for.

Account to delete:

Deletion is final. It cannot be undone.

Your Panier account is deleted. What Panier keeps on your phone is erased by uninstalling the app.

From the app

  1. Open Panier and sign in.
  2. Tap the account icon, at the top right of any of the four tabs (Panier, Évolutions, Produits, Tickets).
  3. Choose Supprimer mon compte ("Delete my account"; the app is in French), then confirm.

Google will ask you to confirm your identity: this is a Firebase security requirement, which refuses to delete an account on an old session.

Without the app

Request the deletion of my account by email

Write to contact.kavini@gmail.com from the address of the account to delete. The request is handled within one month. This route deletes the account at Firebase; what Panier keeps on the phone is erased by uninstalling the app.

What is deleted

  • Your account at Firebase Authentication, with your email address, your name, the address of your profile picture and your identifiers: immediately and permanently. The account ceases to exist at once; Google finishes erasing its data from its systems, backups included, within 180 days at most.
  • Your price history, your receipts and their photographs or PDFs, which are stored only on your phone: erased from that phone at the moment of deletion from the app. Nothing of it is kept elsewhere, and nothing can be recovered.

What is not deleted

  • A copy of a file that the app was receiving or reading at the moment it was interrupted may remain in its cache. The system may empty it on its own when the phone is short of space; uninstalling erases it.
  • What concerns ML Kit on Android, which stays attached to the installation rather than to the account: your choice (a small setting on the phone) and, if ML Kit was used, its installation identifier and what it had queued. It is erased by uninstalling the app; the identifier and the queue are also erased when you withdraw your agreement, as the privacy policy says (the choice then records your withdrawal).
  • What Panier left on another phone, if you used it there or if your history was transferred there: it is erased by uninstalling the app on that phone.
  • The technical data that Google's components send to Google on their own, including the IP addresses of your connections, which Google states it keeps for a few weeks. Panier does not hold it; the privacy policy describes it.
